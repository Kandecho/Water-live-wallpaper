The classic wallpaper/ embeds original image bytes in assets.js, unchanged.
wallpaper-hd/ embeds offline-reconstructed derivatives of the pond and leaves,
plus derived canopy, contact and shadow maps. See wallpaper-hd/NOTICE.txt and
docs/background-upscale.txt and docs/leaf-upscale.txt for HD modifications.
preview.jpg is a screenshot of the desktop adaptation. The original thumbnail is preserved in reference/original-assets/water_thumb.jpg in the source repository.
No assets or rendering code from Reborn Android Live Wallpapers were copied.

Changes from RenderScript:
  WebGL 1 renderer and JavaScript CPU ripple mesh.
  Fixed 30 ms simulation step with time-scaled rotation.
  Bounds-safe normal computation at mesh edges.
  Upright widescreen framing with aspect-preserving center crop.
  Fourteen leaves distributed across one desktop viewport; no Android launcher
  pages, horizontal launcher scrolling, or 90-degree landscape rotation.
  Texture sampling constrained to the image region (excluding source padding).
  Desktop click/touch input, keyboard pause, and engine lifecycle hooks.

This is an independent recreation, not an official Samsung or Google product.
