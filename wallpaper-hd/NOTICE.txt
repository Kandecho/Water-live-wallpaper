This study adds a new compact analytic wave profile with exact derivatives,
shared water/leaf lighting, a soft contact-shadow atlas created at startup,
premultiplied texture sampling, a gentle analytic flow field, smoothed leaf
response, drag input, and time-based animation up to 60 FPS.
Study 02 also derives a padded distance/gradient atlas from the original alpha and
uses a local meniscus gradient to distort a GPU copy of the rendered water.
It is not intended as an exact reproduction of the original RenderScript.
No new HD leaf artwork, fluid solver or physical soft-body simulation is used.
The separately maintained classic version remains available in wallpaper/.
